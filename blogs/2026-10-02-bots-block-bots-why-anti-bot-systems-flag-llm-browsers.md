LLM-driven browsers are browsers and fetchers that a language model operates on a person's behalf. Examples include Perplexity's Comet, ChatGPT's browsing, Claude computer use, and your own Playwright MCP agent. Anti-bot systems from Cloudflare, DataDome, Akamai, and HUMAN inspect and classify that traffic.
