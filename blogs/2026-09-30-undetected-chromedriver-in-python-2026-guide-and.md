The undetected_chromedriver Python package, uc for short, patches ChromeDriver to help Selenium avoid bot detection. In our tests, uc 3.5.5 suppressed 2 common ChromeDriver signals: navigator.webdriver and the injected cdc_ variables. When we tested, the newest PyPI release was 3.5.5, from February 2024.
